ABOUT HMCRI

A fictional institution with a real-world feel.

Himora Medical College & Research Institute is an imaginary institution in the fictional city of Himora, Uttarakhand. This website is a practice project and all people, programmes, statistics and institutional details are fictional.

Our fictional vision is to develop competent, ethical and compassionate healthcare professionals through education, research and patient care.
